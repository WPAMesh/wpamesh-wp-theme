Western Pennsylvania · Off-grid LoRa radio

Mesh networking
for the Yinzers

A community-built radio network centered on Pittsburgh and reaching into Allegheny, Westmoreland, Butler, Armstrong, Indiana and Beaver counties. Radios talking to radios. No cell service or subscription needed.

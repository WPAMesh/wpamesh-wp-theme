Hardware guide

Radios and gear we recommend

What our regulars actually use, for handhelds, repeaters and antennas, plus known problems to watch for. These are opinions from local members, not lab tests.

Companion radios

Wio Tracker L1

Daily carry

Several people’s daily carry. External antenna connector and good battery life. A 1 W version is on the way.

Heltec T096

Best value

The best value. Fully assembled with case, battery, GPS and antenna.

More in Choosing a MeshCore radio →

SenseCAP MeshTracker X1

Card-sized

Credit-card sized, dual-band GPS, IP66. Often backordered.

Heltec RD-GA01

Mentioned as a 22 dBm option.

Repeaters

Building your own

Heltec T096

Most votes

Good transmit power and receive performance at low power draw. The most votes from our regulars.

RAK4631

Low power

Very low power draw. A long-standing choice for solar repeaters.

Prebuilt

PeakMesh Ultimate

Recommended

Described as very solid, with one running 6+ months without issues.

PeakMesh Tree Node

Mixed reports

Works, but members have reported some odd issues.

Power and enclosures

  • BMS boards: PeakMesh’s are good. Watch the polarity when wiring.
  • Cases: anything weatherproof works, printed or bought.
Size solar for a Pennsylvania winter

Repeater kits often ship with too little solar panel for our winters. Plan for short days and a week of clouds. The mesh guide’s Power and solar page covers sizing.

Antennas

ALFA 915 MHz 5 dBi

Fixed

For fixed nodes. Good gain, cost-effective and compact for a fixed antenna. Too bulky for portable use.

Muzi Works 17 cm whip

Portable

For portable radios. Gizont sells what appears to be the same antenna.

Known issues

RadioProblemWhat to do
Heltec V4 and other SX1262 boardsCan go deaf after a strong nearby signalMeshtastic 2.7.20+ resets the radio automatically. On MeshCore 1.14+, set agc.reset.interval 4 (companions need 1.17+)
Heltec V4Receive amplifier (LNA) can overload near strong signalsHeltec suggests disabling it in RF-busy spots. MeshCore already does
Meshnology W12No TCXO, so the frequency can drift off at MeshCore’s narrow bandwidthPrefer a TCXO board for repeaters. Board notes
SenseCAP Solar Node P1 / P1-ProNeeds its own build target. Generic XIAO nRF52840 builds are wired differentlyErase flash before switching firmware, and don’t update over NRF-OTA

“Don’t use a V4 as a companion” is older advice. With current firmware and the AGC reset turned on, it should be fine.

Worth watching

Interesting, but nobody here has run them long enough to recommend:

  • Muzi Base Duo: two radios, LiFePO4 support, SF5 and GFSK
  • RadioCore: said to sit between a RAK4631 and a T114 v2 for efficiency
  • Seeed XIAO ESP32 and nRF52 boards

Firmware matters as much as hardware for battery life. See the community firmware table.

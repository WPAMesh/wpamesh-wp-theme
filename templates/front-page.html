Our fastest-growing network. Dedicated repeaters do the relaying, so your handheld stays quiet and the airwaves hold up as more people join.

Meshtastic

The network WPAmesh started on. Large and well covered, running the Long_Fast preset on the default channel.

  • Use the CLIENT_MUTE role for everyday nodes
  • MQTT gateways bridge isolated areas
  • Good if you already own a Meshtastic radio
